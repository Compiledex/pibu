# Pibu

Clickable prototype of **Pibu** (피부, "skin"), an AI skincare advisor for visitors to Korea. Built for a team Regional Report Presentation on South Korea.

**Live:** https://compiledex.github.io/pibu/

## What it shows

1. Consent: separate photo consent and an AI notice
2. Photo: reads a demo face, and asks again if the photo is too dark
3. Five quick questions
4. A morning and evening routine with prices, store aisle, "Why this?" sources and a labelled ad
5. A Korean ingredient label translated and checked
6. Chat answers with sources
7. "3 weeks later": the "did it work?" check-in that the app learns from

English and Japanese. Products and brands are fictional, AI answers are scripted, and no photo is uploaded anywhere.

## Demo controls

Keys 1–9 jump between screens, R resets, L switches language. On a phone, triple-tap the pibu logo for the same controls.

One self-contained HTML file, no build step.
